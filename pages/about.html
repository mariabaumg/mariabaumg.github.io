About Me.

I'm a senior at Rutgers–New Brunswick double majoring in computer science and mathematics, with a minor in philosophy. After finishing my bachelor's in December 2026, I plan to stay at Rutgers for a master's in statistics through the 4+1 program. I'm especially interested in how we can draw reliable conclusions from noisy data, a question I get to explore in my research on experimental design.

I spent summer 2025 studying combinatorics and number theory through the Budapest Semesters in Math program. At Rutgers, I've participated in the Directed Reading Program, working with a graduate mentor and giving a final talk. I've also worked as a grader for the Mathematics Department.

Outside of school, I like reading, thrifting, watching movies, and playing chess. I keep a small bookshelf here, along with a few travel scraps from places I've been.

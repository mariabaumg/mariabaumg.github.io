NYC Citi Bike Demand Prediction

A team project predicting bike inflow across 1,907 NYC Citi Bike stations. I trained a neural network on 5.5 million hourly records, designed a calendar-based split with a cold-start procedure to prevent temporal leakage across the training-test boundary, and built an interactive map using predictions saved as JSON.

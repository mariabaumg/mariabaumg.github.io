Robust Design

I have the privilege of being advised by Professor Tirthankar Dasgupta on Taguchi methods for robust design. Our project examines how the initial design and initial-sample proportion affect the statistical efficiency of a sequential experiment. Through a full-factorial simulation study in Python, I investigate how these choices perform across different model parameters and noise levels.

We are developing this work toward a manuscript intended for publication.
